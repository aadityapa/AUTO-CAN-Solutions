Where It Began

A decade of engineering trust, one vertical at a time.

Incepted in Jaipur in 2013 as a specialist provider of Hardware-in-the-Loop (HiL) testing, AUTO-CAN steadily broadened its capabilities across the full automotive software development lifecycle.

Company background

AUTO-CAN Solutions was established in 2013 with a clear mission: to bring specialized, high-quality engineering support to the automotive embedded systems industry.

The company started as a niche player in vehicle Hardware-in-the-Loop testing — a discipline critical to validating electronic control units (ECUs) before they reach production vehicles. From this focused beginning, it built deep domain expertise along the way.
